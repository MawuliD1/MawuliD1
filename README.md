# Mawuli Dogbatse

**Electrical Engineer | Telecommunications Researcher | AI/ML & Cybersecurity**

I am an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher from Ghana with a First Class Honours BSc in Telecommunications Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST). My work connects electrical engineering, wireless communications, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and intelligent connected systems.

My research direction is **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I am interested in building and evaluating practical methods that improve the security, reliability and performance of communication and industrial systems.

- **Next-generation wireless systems:** 5G and emerging 6G, massive MIMO, deep-learning-based precoding, channel state information (CSI) compression, NOMA and network optimisation.
- **Integrated sensing and communications:** STAR-RIS, ambiguity-domain signal design, covert communication and sensing-performance optimisation.
- **Resilient IoT and edge systems:** LoRa mesh networking, low-power communications, secure embedded architectures, environmental monitoring and intelligent automation.
- **Cybersecurity and digital investigations:** network security, digital forensics, cybercrime investigation, open-source intelligence and security operations.
- **Electrical and power engineering:** industrial electrical systems, RF and antenna engineering, power-system modelling, resilient microgrids and smart grids.

## Selected research and technical projects

### Wireless communications and intelligent networks
- **STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication:** ambiguity-domain signal hiding, temporal phase randomisation and Riemannian-manifold alternating optimisation. Reported results include a -13.47 dB peak sidelobe level, a 1.77 dB margin below the radar peak sidelobe level, minimum detection error probability of 0.919, and covert throughput of 8.13 bits/symbol.
- **Massive MIMO downlink precoding and CSI compression:** research collaboration on Transformer-based precoding and self-attention for CSI compression, targeting improved spectral efficiency and reduced CSI overhead.
- **DDoS detection in SDN-enabled 5G networks:** undergraduate thesis research using DNN, LSTM and SVM models in a multiple-controller simulated architecture. Reported model accuracies were 99.48%, 99.57% and 98.89%, respectively.

### AI, cybersecurity and edge intelligence
- **Audio deepfake detection:** a 547,000-parameter on-device CNN for mobile banking voice authentication, reported at 4.962 ms CPU inference latency, 9.84% clean EER and 0.60 MB INT8 model size.
- **Crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation:** machine-learning approaches to identifying and mitigating cryptographic ransomware.
- **AI-powered surveillance threat assessment:** YOLO object detection with C3D/I3D action recognition and automated alert notifications.

### Resilient IoT and engineering systems
- **LoRa mesh networking for forest conservation:** ns-3 Layer-2 forwarding, sequence deduplication and randomised backoff. The evaluated scenario reported 100% packet delivery over an 800 m obstructed corridor using five nodes.
- **RF and antenna engineering:** 5 GHz patch antenna design and simulation in Ansys HFSS, including radiation-pattern, S11 and VSWR evaluation.
- **IoT and energy conservation:** smart energy metering and sensor-actuator automated irrigation.
- **NOMA:** analysis of spectrum efficiency and data-throughput improvement techniques.
