# Mawuli Dogbatse

**Electrical Engineer | Telecommunications Researcher | AI/ML & Cybersecurity**

I am an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher with a First Class Honours degree in Telecommunications Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. My work connects engineering fundamentals, wireless communications, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and practical technology development.

My research direction is **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I am interested in methods that improve the security, reliability, efficiency and resilience of connected systems.

## Research Interests

- **AI/ML for Secure Communications:** deep learning, anomaly detection, intelligent threat assessment and robust model evaluation.
- **Intelligent Network Optimisation:** network performance analysis, traffic engineering, SDN and data-driven optimisation.
- **5G and 6G Wireless Systems:** massive MIMO, multi-antenna communications, CSI feedback and compression.
- **Integrated Sensing and Communications:** STAR-RIS-assisted systems, sensing performance, covert communication and signal design.
- **IoT and Edge Intelligence:** resilient low-power networks, LoRa mesh systems, secure embedded systems and connected sensing.
- **Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics:** network security, cybercrime investigation, digital evidence, security operations and OSINT.
- **Electrical and Power Systems:** industrial electrical systems, power distribution, motor control, smart grids and resilient microgrids.
- **RF and Antenna Engineering:** antenna design, electromagnetic simulation and wireless propagation.
