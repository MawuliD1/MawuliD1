# Mawuli Dogbatse

**Electrical Engineer | Telecommunications Researcher | AI/ML & Cybersecurity**

I am an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher with a First Class Honours degree in Telecommunications Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. My work brings together engineering fundamentals, wireless communications, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and practical technology development.

My research direction is **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I am interested in building and evaluating methods that improve the security, reliability, efficiency and resilience of connected systems, while maintaining strong research integrity and practical engineering relevance.

## Research Interests

- **AI and Machine Learning for Secure Communications:** deep learning, anomaly detection, intelligent threat assessment and robust model evaluation.
- **Intelligent Network Optimisation:** network performance analysis, traffic engineering, SDN architectures and data-driven optimisation.
- **5G and 6G Wireless Systems:** massive MIMO, multi-antenna communications, CSI feedback and compression, and emerging wireless architectures.
- **Integrated Sensing and Communications:** STAR-RIS-assisted systems, sensing performance, covert communication and signal design.
- **IoT and Edge Intelligence:** resilient low-power networks, LoRa mesh systems, secure embedded systems and connected sensing.
- **Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics:** network security, cybercrime investigation, digital evidence, security operations and OSINT.
- **Electrical and Power Systems:** industrial electrical systems, power distribution, motor control, smart grids and resilient microgrids.
- **RF and Antenna Engineering:** antenna design, electromagnetic simulation and wireless propagation.

## Selected Research and Technical Work

### Wireless communications and intelligent systems
- STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication using ambiguity-domain signal hiding and temporal phase randomisation.
- Deep learning-based downlink precoding for multi-antenna users in massive MIMO systems, including Transformer-based and self-attention approaches to CSI compression.
- Design and evaluation of a resilient low-power LoRa mesh network for remote forest conservation.
- Non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA) techniques for enhanced wireless communication.
- RF and antenna design, including 5 GHz antenna simulation using Ansys HFSS.

### AI, cybersecurity and digital forensics
- Machine-learning-based DDoS detection and mitigation in SDN-enabled 5G mobile networks with multiple controllers.
- Crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation using machine learning.
- AI-powered surveillance threat assessment and autonomous security alerting using object detection and action recognition.
- Lightweight on-device CNN research for audio deepfake detection in mobile banking applications.

### Electrical engineering and IoT
- Industrial electrical systems, MV/LV equipment, switchgear, motor control and maintenance support.
- IoT-based energy monitoring and conservation.
- Automated sensor-actuator irrigation architecture.
