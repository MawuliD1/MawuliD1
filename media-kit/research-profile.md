# Research Profile

## Research direction

My research direction is **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I combine interests in communication theory, machine learning, network engineering and cybersecurity to develop methods that can be rigorously evaluated and applied to practical systems.

## Core research themes

### AI and machine learning for secure communications
- Deep-learning and machine-learning methods for network anomaly and threat detection.
- DDoS detection and mitigation in software-defined and 5G network environments.
- Crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation using machine learning.
- Lightweight neural networks for audio deepfake detection and mobile-device deployment.
- Model robustness, generalisation, computational efficiency and responsible evaluation.

### Intelligent network optimisation and next-generation wireless
- 5G and emerging 6G network architectures.
- Massive MIMO and multi-antenna communication systems.
- Deep-learning-based downlink precoding.
- Transformer and self-attention methods for channel state information (CSI) compression.
- CSI feedback overhead, spectral efficiency and wireless performance optimisation.
- NOMA and resource-efficient wireless communication.

### Integrated sensing, communication and covert transmission
- STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and communication.
- Ambiguity-domain signal design and signal hiding.
- Temporal phase randomisation and sensing-performance trade-offs.
- Covert communication performance and detection analysis.

### Resilient IoT, LoRa and edge intelligence
- Low-power and long-range wireless mesh networking.
- Resilient communication for remote environmental monitoring and conservation.
- Secure embedded and edge-connected architectures.
- IoT sensing, energy monitoring and automation.

### Cybersecurity, digital forensics and threat assessment
- Network security monitoring and incident analysis.
- Digital forensic investigation and evidence-handling principles.
- Cybercrime investigation and open-source intelligence.
- AI-supported surveillance threat assessment and action recognition.
- Security operations and digital safety education.

### Electrical, power and RF engineering
- Power-system modelling for resilient microgrids and smart grids.
- Industrial electrical systems, distribution, switchgear and motor control.
- RF and antenna design and electromagnetic simulation.
- Wireless propagation, link analysis, testing and troubleshooting.

## Selected research contributions

- STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication using ambiguity-domain signal hiding and temporal phase randomisation.
- Lightweight on-device CNN for real-time audio deepfake detection in mobile banking applications.
- Resilient low-power LoRa mesh network for remote forest conservation.
- Machine-learning-based DDoS detection and mitigation in SDN-enabled 5G mobile networks with multiple controllers.
- Transformer-based downlink precoding and self-attention CSI compression for massive MIMO.
- Machine-learning approaches to crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation.
- AI-powered surveillance threat assessment using YOLO and C3D/I3D action-recognition approaches.

## Methods, platforms and tools

My work may involve analytical modelling, simulation, controlled experimentation, dataset analysis, machine-learning development and performance evaluation. Tools and platforms used across my projects include Python, MATLAB, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, Mininet, Ryu, OpenFlow, ns-3, Linux, Wireshark, Ansys HFSS, Advanced Design System (ADS), MATLAB/Simulink, Multisim and LTspice.

## Collaboration interests

I welcome research discussions and collaborations in wireless communications, AI/ML, network security, massive MIMO, integrated sensing and communication, digital forensics, IoT, RF systems and resilient power and connected infrastructure.

Potential collaboration formats include reproducible simulation studies, algorithm development, model evaluation, system prototyping, literature reviews and interdisciplinary engineering research.

Research themes describe areas of contribution and interest. They should not be interpreted as evidence that every topic is a completed publication or deployed operational system.
