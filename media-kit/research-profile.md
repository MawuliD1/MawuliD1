# Research Profile

## Research direction

My research interests focus on **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I am interested in combining communication theory, machine learning, network engineering and cybersecurity to develop methods that can be evaluated rigorously and translated into practical systems.

## Research themes

### 1. AI and machine learning for secure networks
- Machine-learning methods for network anomaly and threat detection.
- DDoS detection and mitigation in software-defined and 5G network environments.
- Learning-based detection of malicious activity, including ransomware-related behaviour.
- Model evaluation, generalisation, robustness and the responsible use of security datasets.

### 2. Wireless communications and next-generation networks
- 5G and emerging 6G network architectures.
- Massive MIMO and multi-antenna communication systems.
- Deep-learning-based downlink precoding.
- Channel state information (CSI) feedback, compression and overhead reduction.
- Wireless performance analysis and intelligent optimisation.

### 3. Secure sensing, IoT and edge intelligence
- Security and privacy considerations in connected sensing systems.
- AI-enabled threat assessment and edge-connected applications.
- Software-defined networking and programmable network architectures.
- Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and emerging integrated sensing and communication concepts.

### 4. Cybersecurity and digital forensics
- Network security monitoring and incident analysis.
- Digital forensic investigation and evidence handling.
- Security operations and threat assessment.
- Practical security education and cyber safety.

### 5. RF and electrical engineering
- RF and antenna design and electromagnetic simulation.
- Electrical systems, distribution and motor-control fundamentals.
- Engineering measurement, testing, troubleshooting and documentation.

## Research methods and tools

Depending on the research question, my work may involve simulation, controlled experimentation, dataset analysis, machine-learning model development and performance evaluation. Tools in my technical workflow include Python, MATLAB, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, Linux, Wireshark, Ansys HFSS and Keysight ADS.

## Current research interests and selected work

- Machine-learning-based DDoS detection and mitigation in SDN-enabled 5G network environments with multiple controllers.
- Transformer and self-attention approaches to CSI compression and deep-learning-based downlink precoding for massive MIMO.
- AI-supported surveillance threat assessment using object detection and action-recognition methods.
- STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing, communication and covert transmission research.

## Collaboration interests

I welcome discussions with researchers and practitioners working in wireless communications, network security, AI/ML, digital forensics, RF systems and IoT. Potential collaboration areas include reproducible simulation studies, model evaluation, system prototyping, literature reviews and interdisciplinary engineering research.

Research descriptions in this document are summaries of areas of work and interest. They should not be interpreted as claims that every listed topic is a completed publication or deployed system.
