# Research Profile

## Research direction

My research direction is **AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems**. I am interested in combining communication theory, machine learning, network engineering and cybersecurity to develop methods that can be rigorously evaluated and applied to practical systems.

## Core research themes

### AI and machine learning for secure communications
- Deep-learning and machine-learning methods for network anomaly and threat detection.
- DDoS detection and mitigation in software-defined and 5G network environments.
- Crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation using machine learning.
- Lightweight neural networks for audio deepfake detection and mobile-device deployment.
- Model robustness, generalisation, computational efficiency and responsible evaluation.

### Next-generation wireless and intelligent network optimisation
- 5G and emerging 6G network architectures.
- Massive MIMO and multi-antenna communication systems.
- Deep-learning-based downlink precoding.
- Transformer and self-attention methods for channel state information (CSI) compression.
- CSI feedback overhead, spectral efficiency and wireless performance optimisation.
- Non-Orthogonal Multiple Access (NOMA), resource allocation and spectrum efficiency.
- Mobile and satellite communications and network planning.

### Integrated sensing, communication and covert transmission
- Simultaneous Transmitting and Reflecting Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (STAR-RIS).
- Integrated sensing and communication and covert communication.
- Ambiguity-domain signal design and signal hiding.
- Temporal phase randomisation and sensing-performance trade-offs.
- Covert communication performance and detection analysis.

### Resilient IoT, LoRa and edge intelligence
- Low-power and long-range wireless mesh networking.
- Resilient communication for remote environmental monitoring and forest conservation.
- Secure embedded and edge-connected architectures.
- IoT sensing, energy monitoring and automation.
- Sensor-actuator systems for agricultural water-use optimisation.

### Cybersecurity, digital forensics and threat assessment
- Network security monitoring and incident analysis.
- Digital forensic investigation and evidence-handling principles.
- Cybercrime investigation and open-source intelligence.
- AI-supported surveillance threat assessment and action recognition.
- Security operations, cyber fraud investigation and digital safety education.

### Electrical, power and RF engineering
- Power-system modelling for resilient microgrids and smart grids.
- Industrial electrical systems, distribution, switchgear and motor control.
- RF and antenna design and electromagnetic simulation.
- Wireless propagation, link analysis, testing and troubleshooting.

## Selected research contributions

1. **STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication:** ambiguity-domain signal hiding, temporal phase randomisation and Riemannian-manifold alternating optimisation.
2. **On-device audio deepfake detection:** compact CNN design and evaluation for mobile banking voice authentication.
3. **Resilient LoRa mesh networking:** low-power multi-hop communications for remote forest conservation.
4. **DDoS detection and mitigation:** machine-learning evaluation in SDN-enabled 5G mobile networks with multiple controllers.
5. **Massive MIMO downlink precoding and CSI compression:** Transformer-based precoding and self-attention CSI processing.
6. **Crypto-ransomware detection and mitigation:** machine-learning approaches to cryptographic ransomware threats.
7. **AI-powered surveillance threat assessment:** YOLO object detection, C3D/I3D action recognition and automated alerts.
8. **NOMA and wireless performance:** analysis of spectrum efficiency and data-throughput improvement techniques.
9. **RF antenna design:** 5 GHz patch antenna modelling and simulation using Ansys HFSS.
10. **IoT energy and agriculture systems:** smart energy metering and automated sensor-actuator irrigation.

## Methods, platforms and tools

My work may involve analytical modelling, simulation, controlled experimentation, dataset analysis, machine-learning development and performance evaluation. Tools and platforms used across my projects include Python, MATLAB, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, Mininet, Ryu, OpenFlow, ns-3, Linux, Wireshark, Ansys HFSS, Advanced Design System (ADS), MATLAB/Simulink, Multisim, LTspice, Cisco Packet Tracer and Huawei eNSP.

## Collaboration interests

I welcome research discussions and collaborations in wireless communications, AI/ML, network security, massive MIMO, integrated sensing and communication, digital forensics, IoT, RF systems, smart grids and resilient connected infrastructure.

Potential collaboration formats include reproducible simulation studies, algorithm development, model evaluation, literature reviews, system prototyping and interdisciplinary engineering research.

Research themes describe areas of contribution and interest. They should not be interpreted as evidence that every topic is a completed publication or deployed operational system.
