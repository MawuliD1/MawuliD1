# Publications and Conference Presentations

## IEEE ICAST 2026

I contributed to the following co-authored papers presented at the **10th IEEE International Conference on Adaptive Science and Technology (ICAST 2026)**, held **22–24 October 2026** at **Academic City University, Accra, Ghana**.

The conference theme was **“Engineering a resilient future through convergent technologies.”**

### 1. STAR-RIS-Assisted Integrated Sensing and Covert Communication via Ambiguity-Domain Signal Hiding and Temporal Phase Randomization

**Authors:** Lawerteh Isaiah; Nafiu Webabe Mohammed; Benjamin Ekow Attabra; Mawuli Dogbatse; Farida Yussif; and Naa Norley Norteye.

**Research area:** STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication, ambiguity-domain signal hiding, temporal phase randomisation and optimisation of sensing/covert-throughput trade-offs.

### 2. A Lightweight On-Device CNN for Real-Time Audio Deepfake Detection in Mobile Banking Applications

**Authors:** Farida Yussif; Isaiah Lawerteh; Nathaniel Agbugblah; Philip Ofori; Shaddrack Yaw Nusenu; Naa Norley Norteye; Isaac Kweku Boakye; Mawuli Dogbatse; and Lisa Azasumah Timbilla.

**Research area:** Lightweight deep-learning architecture, audio deepfake detection, mobile banking voice authentication and robustness evaluation under codec and noise conditions.

### 3. Design and Evaluation of a Resilient Low-Power LoRa Mesh Network for Remote Forest Conservation

**Authors:** Nafiu Webabe Mohammed; Isaac Kweku Boakye; Isaiah Lawerteh; Mawuli Dogbatse; Abdul-Jawad Iddrisu; John Nyamekye Dadzie; and Kwara Modesta Kayaga.

**Research area:** Low-power LoRa mesh networking, propagation modelling, terrain and foliage obstruction, multi-hop forwarding and packet-delivery evaluation for remote forest monitoring.
