# Research and Professional Collaboration

I welcome thoughtful opportunities to collaborate on engineering research, telecommunications, AI/ML, cybersecurity and technology development.

## Areas of collaboration

- AI/ML methods for secure communications and network optimisation.
- 5G/6G wireless systems, massive MIMO and CSI processing.
- SDN security, network measurement and simulation.
- Digital forensics, security education and cyber safety.
- RF engineering, IoT and intelligent connected systems.

## Potential forms of collaboration

Depending on the objectives and available resources, I am interested in:
- Joint research and technical publications.
- Reproducible simulation and experimental studies.
- Research assistance and technical development.
- Student mentoring and knowledge-sharing activities.
- Engineering prototypes and proof-of-concept systems.
- Technical reviews and interdisciplinary project discussions.

## What to include in an enquiry

To help assess a proposed collaboration, please provide:

1. Your name, role and institutional or organisational affiliation.
2. A short description of the research question or project.
3. The expected contribution and responsibilities of each collaborator.
4. Proposed timeline, milestones and expected outputs.
5. Available datasets, equipment, compute resources or funding, where relevant.
6. Any authorship, intellectual-property, confidentiality or data-governance requirements.

## Research integrity and working principles

I value clear communication, accurate attribution, reproducible methods, responsible data handling and transparent reporting of limitations. Authorship should reflect substantive scholarly contributions and be agreed by collaborators in accordance with relevant institutional and publication policies.

No confidential, personal, proprietary or restricted material should be shared before appropriate approvals and safeguards are in place. Do not send passwords, access tokens or sensitive datasets through public channels.
