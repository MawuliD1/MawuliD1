# Professional Biography

## Short biography

Mawuli Dogbatse is an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher from Ghana. He holds a First Class Honours BSc in Telecommunications Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), with a Cumulative Weighted Average of 73.92/100. His interests span artificial intelligence and machine learning, secure communications, wireless networks, cybersecurity, digital forensics, IoT and industrial electrical systems.

His research and technical work include machine-learning-based DDoS detection in SDN-enabled 5G networks, Transformer-based approaches to massive-MIMO precoding and CSI compression, STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication, lightweight audio deepfake detection, resilient LoRa mesh networking and AI-supported surveillance threat assessment. He has contributed to three co-authored research papers presented at IEEE ICAST 2026 in Accra, Ghana.

Alongside research, he has experience in university teaching and student mentoring, telecommunications network simulation and optimisation, and industrial electrical engineering. He is particularly interested in advancing AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems.

## Extended biography

Mawuli Dogbatse is an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher whose work connects communication systems, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and practical engineering. He graduated from KNUST with First Class Honours in Telecommunications Engineering, achieving a CWA of 73.92/100.

His research interests include AI and machine learning for secure communications, intelligent network optimisation, 5G and emerging 6G wireless systems, massive MIMO, channel-state-information processing, integrated sensing and communications, resilient IoT networks, digital forensics and secure embedded edge architectures. He is also interested in power-system modelling for resilient microgrids and smart grids, RF engineering and industrial electrical systems.

His research contributions include STAR-RIS-assisted integrated sensing and covert communication through ambiguity-domain signal hiding and temporal phase randomisation; a lightweight on-device convolutional neural network for audio deepfake detection in mobile banking; and a low-power LoRa mesh network for remote forest conservation. These three co-authored papers were presented at the 10th IEEE International Conference on Adaptive Science and Technology (ICAST 2026) in Accra, Ghana. Other research and project experience includes DDoS detection and mitigation in SDN-enabled 5G networks, deep-learning-based downlink precoding and CSI compression for massive MIMO, crypto-ransomware detection, AI-powered surveillance threat assessment, NOMA analysis, RF antenna design and IoT systems.

Professionally, he has worked in industrial electrical engineering at Tema Oil Refinery, technology leadership at Cyber Secured Africa, and academic teaching and research support at KNUST. Earlier experience includes network simulation and optimisation at MTN Ghana and systems support at ENABLE IT. His academic work included undergraduate and Distance-Learning teaching, laboratory support, tutorials, assessment and student mentoring.

He contributes to engineering and technology communities through IEEE activities, internet governance and digital safety advocacy, student leadership and professional development. His recognition includes the Gunther and Shirley Karger Chapter and Community Leadership Award, IEEE Communications Society Student Ambassador appointment and the Ghana School on Internet Governance Fellowship.

He welcomes meaningful research and professional collaboration grounded in technical rigour, responsible innovation, accurate attribution and reproducible work.
