# Professional Biography

## Short biography

Mawuli Dogbatse is an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher whose interests include artificial intelligence and machine learning, wireless communications, cybersecurity and intelligent network systems. He holds a First Class Honours BSc in Telecommunications Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. His technical interests connect engineering fundamentals with data-driven approaches to improving the security, reliability and performance of connected systems.

His work and research interests span machine-learning-based network security, software-defined networking, 5G and emerging 6G systems, massive MIMO, channel-state-information processing, digital forensics, RF engineering and industrial electrical systems. He has been involved in academic teaching and research support, telecommunications network simulation and optimisation, and engineering and cybersecurity initiatives.

He is particularly interested in research and practical development at the intersection of AI-driven security and intelligent network optimisation for next-generation wireless and IoT systems.

## Extended biography

Mawuli Dogbatse is an Electrical Engineer and telecommunications researcher based in Ghana. He graduated from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology with a First Class Honours degree in Telecommunications Engineering.

His academic and technical interests centre on the design, analysis and improvement of connected systems. These include wireless communication networks, machine learning for network security, software-defined networking, massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems, intelligent network optimisation, digital forensics and RF engineering. He is also interested in the practical engineering of industrial electrical systems, including electrical distribution, switchgear, motor control and maintenance.

His project experience includes research into machine-learning-based detection and mitigation of distributed denial-of-service activity in SDN-enabled 5G network environments, AI-supported surveillance threat assessment, ransomware detection, deep-learning approaches to massive-MIMO downlink precoding and channel-state-information compression, and RF and IoT engineering projects. These projects are described according to their actual research or implementation status, with limitations and permissions respected.

Alongside technical work, he contributes to teaching, student development, professional engineering communities and digital safety advocacy. He welcomes opportunities to collaborate on technically sound research, engineering development and responsible applications of emerging technologies.
