# Speaking and Technical Engagements

## Areas I can contribute to

I am open to suitable opportunities to contribute to technical discussions, student development activities, workshops, panels and professional learning sessions within my areas of knowledge and experience.

Potential topics include:

### AI, machine learning and network security
- Introduction to machine learning for network security.
- Understanding DDoS threats in software-defined network environments.
- Building and evaluating machine-learning experiments for cybersecurity.
- Responsible interpretation of AI-based surveillance and threat-detection systems.

### Telecommunications and wireless engineering
- Foundations of 5G networks and emerging 6G research directions.
- Introduction to massive MIMO and multi-antenna communication systems.
- CSI feedback and the role of machine learning in wireless systems.
- Network simulation and optimisation for engineering students.

### Cybersecurity and digital safety
- Cyber hygiene and online safety awareness.
- Introduction to digital forensics and evidence-handling principles.
- Cybersecurity learning pathways for engineering students and early-career professionals.

### Electrical and engineering practice
- Engineering problem-solving and technical documentation.
- Introduction to RF and antenna simulation workflows.
- Connecting engineering fundamentals with practical laboratory work.

## Formats

Depending on the subject and audience, I can consider:
- Technical talks and guest lectures.
- Student and early-career engineering workshops.
- Conference and professional panels.
- Research discussions and seminars.
- Demonstrations based on non-confidential laboratory or simulation work.

## Requesting a contribution

Please include the following information in an initial enquiry:

- Organisation, institution or event name.
- Proposed date, time zone and location or online platform.
- Intended audience and expected number of participants.
- Proposed topic, format and expected duration.
- Whether the session will be recorded, photographed or published.
- Any preparation, travel, accessibility or technical requirements.
- Whether the engagement is voluntary, funded or otherwise compensated.

I will review each request based on subject fit, availability, preparation requirements and any applicable institutional or professional commitments.

## Accuracy note

The topics above describe areas in which I may be able to contribute. They are not a list of past speaking engagements or evidence of invitations. Confirmed appearances will be added only after they have taken place or when an event has formally announced them with permission.
